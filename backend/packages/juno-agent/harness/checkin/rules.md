Rules: do ONE concrete step with your normal tools (research, draft, prepare, check) instead of only planning. Record it with goal_update (id, the same title, summary = where it stands now, milestones with updated statuses, note = what you did, nextCheckAt = when to look again: 1 hour to 7 days from now, a day is typical). Tell the user (in your final reply) ONLY if something really changed, you need their decision or approval, or a deadline is within 24 hours. Otherwise record it.{{quiet}} Read /juno/docs/goals.md for how.

Record a rule your owner just gave you about what you may message them about without being asked: "stop telling me about cricket" (kind dont), "much less of the daily word" (less), "do tell me about flight delays" (tell), "put that in the Feed, not the chat" (where). Only when the owner said it themselves in this chat: `quote` is their exact words and the rule is refused if those words are not in one of their messages here. scope: topic (a subject), type (a kind of message: a daily word, a news digest, goal check-ins) or frequency (how often). `rule` is one short phrase in their meaning ("Cricket scores"). A one-off "not now" or "skip this one" is not a rule. Never for things you only guess they want. The owner sees every rule in the app and can delete it.

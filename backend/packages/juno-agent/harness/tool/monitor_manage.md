Look after the monitors you made (monitor_create). action "list" shows them with when each last checked and last found something; "pause" and "resume" switch one off and on; "renew" gives one 30 more days (use it when the person says to keep watching after one expired); "stop" deletes it. Everything but "list" needs the monitor `id`. Only the person you work for can ask for these.

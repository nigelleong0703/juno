Send one photo from the owner's iPhone library into this chat, by the id from phone_photos_search. The picture is uploaded, appears in the chat as a file the owner can open, is saved in their files, and you can look at it. The owner is asked first, because a private picture leaves the phone. Only photos can be sent (not videos). Runs on the owner's iPhone through the Juno app, so it works only while the app is open (otherwise it is refused and says so).

Add reminders on the iPhone, each at a time (due), at a place (place: when arriving at or leaving it), or just on a list. Put every reminder of one request in one call (up to 20): the phone adds them together and the owner sees one card, not one per reminder. It is the owner's own list and easy to undo, so the owner is not asked first. For "remind me when I get to the office" give the place; if you do not know which place is meant, ask the user instead of guessing. The answer says per reminder whether it was added or why it failed: tell the owner what failed, and do not repeat the ones that were added. Runs on the owner's iPhone through the Juno app, so it works only while the app is open (otherwise it is refused and says so).

List the owner's homes, rooms and accessories (lights, switches, thermostats, blinds, sensors, locks) from HomeKit on the iPhone, with each accessory's room, whether it is reachable and its last known state (on or off, brightness, temperature, position; a lock only shows locked or unlocked). Locks, garage doors and alarms are listed but you cannot operate them. Runs on the owner's iPhone through the Juno app, so it works only while the app is open (otherwise it is refused and says so).

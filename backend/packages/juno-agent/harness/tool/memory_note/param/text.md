The note: one short line, self-contained, at most 300 characters. Write each item as one short, self-contained statement in the language the person writes in. About the person being helped, leave the subject out and write in their own register: "Wants to build muscle and cut sugar", "Prefers warm, normal-length replies", "Lives in Leiden". Never begin with "User", "The user" or "You" (other assistants read these lines, and "You" would read as the assistant itself). About anyone else, begin with their name: "Priya manages Tom", "Lily loves sushi". Never write "I", "me" or "my" without saying whose they are.

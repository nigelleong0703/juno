Where the note belongs on their memory page. Topic, one of: identity (who they are: name, age, origin, languages, background, beliefs); health (body and wellbeing: food, fitness, sleep, medical); work (job, business, projects, clients, studies, money from work); plans (things they intend or promised to do: trips, goals, dates, commitments); preferences (tastes and how they want things done, including how assistants should talk to them); people (family, friends, colleagues and how they relate to them); agents (what they want from their assistants or how the assistants work for them); home (where they live, household, belongings, routines at home); other (anything that fits none of the above).

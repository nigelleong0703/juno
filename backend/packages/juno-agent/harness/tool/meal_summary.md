Read the food diary for a range of the user's local dates (from and to as YYYY-MM-DD, inclusive, at most 92 days): per-day totals (kcal, protein, carbs, fat) and each entry with its id. Use it to answer "what did I eat today", "how was my week", or before correcting an entry. Days are the user's own days, so a late-night snack belongs to the day it was eaten. The numbers are mostly estimates; say so when it matters.

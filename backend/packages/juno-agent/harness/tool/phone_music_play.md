Start playing a song, album or playlist on the owner's iPhone, replacing what is in the queue. It plays from Apple Music when that is available (needs the owner's subscription), otherwise from the owner's own music library. The answer says what was found and is now playing; check it matches what the user asked for. If it says the title is not in the library, tell the owner that plainly and do not try other ways to play it. Not asked first. Runs on the owner's iPhone through the Juno app, so it works only while the app is open (otherwise it is refused and says so); the music keeps playing after the app closes.

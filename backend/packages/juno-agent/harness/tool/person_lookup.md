Look up one person in the owner's life: what they recently said that you may repeat here, and, when you are talking with the owner alone, their accounts, how they are connected to other people, and possible matches. Give a name or a person id (per_...). To follow a connection, look up the other person next, e.g. look up Priya, then look up her manager by name. Only what the people here may hear is shown; if nothing is, you are told so. Possible matches are accounts with that name that are not linked to the person. Each one lists the latest messages that account wrote and the chat they are in: present it as: a Tom in <chat> said (quote one message), is that the Tom you mean? Link it with person_link only if the owner confirms. Never link on your own.

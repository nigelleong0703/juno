Offer the user one idea for a next step; nothing happens until they accept (`prompt` is what they would send you). Use it after finishing a task when you saw a concrete next step they did not ask for yet, or when you noticed something needing attention (a deadline, an unanswered message, a stalled goal); never in the middle of someone else's request, never to fill silence, at most one or two per conversation. A good idea names its source (the goal, conversation or memory line), is something you can really do with your tools right now (not negotiating, buying or calling without a tool for it), is specific and finished in one sentence ("Draft a reply to Priya about the Friday move"), and is not already waiting or dismissed. When the work is writing the user will review, draft it first and offer to send or refine it; never say it was sent. Do not suggest when your settings say not to reach out, or what a goal already tracks.

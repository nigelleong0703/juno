Ask the person ONE plain question about an outside action you were told may or may not have happened (a tool result says so). Use it only after you have checked with a read tool and still cannot tell, AND doing the action a second time would have a real consequence (a second email, a second payment, a duplicate booking). If you can tell, or a repeat would be harmless, do not ask: decide. The action is the last one you were told about; you do not name it. Write the question in the person's language, in everyday words about what could happen, e.g. "The email to Bob may already have gone out and I can't see it. Send it again?". Never mention tools, parameters, ids or times. The question shows with two buttons; the answer arrives as their next message, with a note on whether you may repeat the action once. End your turn after asking and do not repeat the action meanwhile.

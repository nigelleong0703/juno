Put one item in YOUR OWN schedule (not the user's Calendar app): a follow-up you promised ("check back on the quote Thursday"), a meeting you will join yourself, or a one-off task for later. At the time you are woken in this chat with the instructions you wrote, so make them complete. Recurring work is a routine, not a schedule item. Check "Your schedule" first to avoid double-booking. Required: kind, title, at (a future time), instructions. endAt and meeting are for kind "meeting" only; leave out any optional field you have no value for.

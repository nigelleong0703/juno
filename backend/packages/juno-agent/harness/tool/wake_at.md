Pause YOUR OWN current work until a time and carry on afterwards (a build, a cool-down, a page that loads later). A wait longer than two minutes ends this turn: you are free to handle other messages and are woken in this chat when the time comes. A short wait holds this run open. Not for a reminder or follow-up you promised the person ("remind me in 10 min", "check back Thursday"): those go in your schedule (schedule_add, found with tool_search "schedule"), which wakes you with the full instructions even though this run has ended.

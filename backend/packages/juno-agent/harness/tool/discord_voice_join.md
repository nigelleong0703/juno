Discord: join a Discord voice channel with your bot to listen and take notes (owner only; you cannot speak). Without channelId you join the voice channel the owner is in right now. Everyone in the channel is transcribed per speaker; you announce the note-taking in the channel's text chat, answer questions mentioning you there, and when you leave (or the channel empties) you are woken once to post notes and action items in this conversation.

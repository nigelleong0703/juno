Create or update one of your goals, the things you keep track of for the person you are helping. The title, summary and note are shown to them in the app and written to them: use "you" and "your" ("Where your Tokyo trip stands"), never "the owner" or "the user". When the person asks for something long-running or to keep track of something, create a goal (no id). kind "tracking" is a concrete watch (a date, a price, a status); kind "goal" is a longer aim worked on in steps. Keep summary to one line saying where it stands now. As work progresses, update summary and steps (pass the full steps list; mark finished ones done). Set status "done" when finished or "paused" when on hold. Your active goals are listed in your context by title: to update one, repeat its exact title (the same title updates, never duplicates) or pass the id this tool returned; to finish it, use the same title with status "done". A goal also keeps an action plan: milestones (3 to 7 steps; pass the full list) and nextCheckAt (when you will look at it again; the platform wakes you then). note is one line on what you just did, for the goal timeline. Only message the user about a goal when something really changed, you need a decision, or a deadline is within 24 hours. Progress made in conversation (practice, coaching, learning together) is recorded by the background chat digest after the chat goes quiet, so do not call goal_update for it. Following up on other things later is done with routines or wake_at.

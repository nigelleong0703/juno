Create or update one of your goals, the things you keep track of for the person you are helping. The title, summary and note are shown to them in the app and are written to them: use "you" and "your" ("Where your Tokyo trip stands"), never "the owner" or "the user". When the user asks for something long-running or to keep track of something, create a goal (no id). kind "tracking" is a concrete watch (a date, a price, a status); kind "goal" is a longer aim worked on in steps. Keep summary to one line saying where it stands now. As work progresses, update summary and steps (pass the full steps list; mark finished ones done). Set status "done" when it is finished or "paused" when it is on hold. Your active goals are listed in your context by title. To update one, repeat its exact title (a goal with the same title is updated, not duplicated), or pass the id this tool returned when it was created; to finish it, use the same title with status "done". For a goal, also keep an action plan: milestones (3 to 7 steps, each {title, status todo|doing|done|blocked, due?}; pass the full list) and nextCheckAt (when you will look at it again, an ISO time between 1 hour and 7 days from now; you are woken then by the platform). Use note for one line saying what you just did; it goes into the goal timeline. Only message the user about a goal when something really changed, you need a decision, or a deadline is within 24 hours. Progress made in conversation with the user (practice, coaching, learning together) is recorded by the background chat digest after the chat goes quiet, so do not call goal_update for that progress. When the person states something they want to work on over time, record it here in that same turn; keep using it for long-running things you track or work on with your tools. Following up on other things later is done with routines or wake_at.

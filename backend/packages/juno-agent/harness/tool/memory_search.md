Search what is remembered that the people in this chat may hear: what the user has said and what was noted about them, the people in their life, and in a group what the group has said. Matches by meaning and by words. Returns each match with who said it, where, and its [mem:...] or [claim:...] tag, which memory_explain takes. Use it before saying you do not know something about the user or the people in their life.

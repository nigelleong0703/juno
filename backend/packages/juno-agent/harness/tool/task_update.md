Keep your task list, which the person you are helping sees in the app (on your Team card too). Every line you write here (title, progress, outcome) is shown to that person and is written to them: use "you" and "your" ("Building your Tokyo plan"), never "the owner", "the user" or their name in the third person. Call it when you start real work for them (not for a plain reply or chit-chat): create a task with no id and title, a short imperative phrase like "Find rain jacket options". While you work, keep progress to one current line ("Checking rain jacket inventory in size small"). Finish it with status "completed" or "failed" and a one-line outcome ("Listed 16 Sept and Oct school dates"); use status "waiting" with an outcome when you asked the user and need their answer ("Asked for a size choice to find rain jackets"), and set it "running" again when you continue. A "failed" outcome says what you saw (what the page or the tool error said, quoted or closely paraphrased), never a cause nothing stated (blocked, bot check, region, out of stock); if you could not tell why, say so. A task left running when your turn ends is closed for you. Long-running or ongoing things to keep track of belong in goal_update instead. Background work you start (child_spawn, shell.start, background calls) is listed by itself: give it a title there.

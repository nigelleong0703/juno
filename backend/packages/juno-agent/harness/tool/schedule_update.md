Change an item of your own schedule by id: retitle it, move it (at, a future time), rewrite the instructions, or record how it went (result, also on a finished item). Moving a scheduled item re-arms its wake-up. Required: id. Give only the fields that change and leave out the rest; send endAt as null to remove a meeting's end time.
